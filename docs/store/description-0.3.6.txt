See your remaining ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok usage without repeatedly opening account settings.

Usage Meter shows the limits reported by your signed-in web sessions. No API keys, CLI tools or separate desktop applications are required.

AT A GLANCE
• A toolbar percentage that follows the active supported tab. Other websites have no percentage badge.
• A popup showing used and remaining percentages together, with time until reset.
• An optional draggable widget showing quotas and reset dates inside the chat page.
• Three widget views: full details, usage bars only, or a compact summary strip.
• Light and dark styling that follows the page, with colour warnings as usage approaches its limit.
• Additional ChatGPT feature counts and limit-reset credits when the service reports them.
• Grok Free request counts and an experimental shared-pool display for paid Grok accounts.

Choose whether the toolbar badge shows used or remaining usage. When the provider reports several main usage windows, the badge alternates between them. The popup and widget always show both values.

ACCOUNT AND DATA HANDLING
The extension reads account identifiers, session authentication, usage metadata and relevant page/network data only to retrieve and display your quotas and keep account caches separate. Processing and storage are local to your browser. Authentication is used only with the corresponding provider. No analytics, advertising trackers or developer-operated data server are included. Relevant chat-response streams may be inspected for quota events; chat messages are not saved or sent to the developer.

PROVIDER DIFFERENCES
Available information depends on your provider and plan. A ChatGPT Free account's reported Work/Codex pool is not a counter for every ChatGPT message. On the Claude Free account tested, the first numeric reading may require a normal chat reply. Gemini readings concern the Gemini web service. Grok Free exposes Fast request counts and the quota window duration, but no exact reset time. Paid Grok support is experimental and has not been tested on a live paid account.

Usage Meter displays provider-reported limits and does not increase or bypass them. It is an independent extension, unaffiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or xAI. Internal website changes can affect compatibility.
